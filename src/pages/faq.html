The product

What is makhana?

Makhana, also called fox nut or phool makhana, is the popped seed of the lotus plant. Roasted, it becomes a light, crunchy snack that is naturally gluten-free.

Is Vohra's makhana fried?

No. Every flavour is dry-roasted with rice bran oil, never deep-fried.

How many flavours are there?

Seven: Cream & Onion, Peri Peri, Tangy Tomato, Mint Pudina, Cheesy Delight, Pink Salt, and Pink Salt & Black Pepper.

How much is in a tin?

Each tin holds 100 g. The label lists a 28 g serving size.

Which flavour has the most protein?

Cheesy Delight, with 11.63 g of protein per 100 g. Tangy Tomato follows at 10.36 g. You can compare all seven on the home page.

Ingredients & allergens

Is it gluten-free?

Yes. Makhana is naturally gluten-free and the packs are labelled gluten-free.

Which flavours contain peanut or milk?

Six flavours contain peanut and milk derivatives. Tangy Tomato also contains soya. Pink Salt has no declared allergens. All flavours are produced in a facility where tree nuts and dairy may be present.

Is Vohra's makhana vegetarian?

Yes, all seven flavours carry the green vegetarian mark.

How should I store an opened tin?

Close the lid after use and keep the tin in a cool, dry, hygienic place away from direct sunlight and heat.
