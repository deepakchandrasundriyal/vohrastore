Returns & refunds

Last updated 9 October 2026 · Draft for client review

Because our products are food, we cannot accept returns of opened or undamaged tins. We will always put things right if something goes wrong.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at {{email}} or {{phone1}}.
  • Share your order ID and a photo of the tin and the outer box.
  • We will send a replacement or issue a refund, whichever you prefer.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge before it ships. Once it has shipped it cannot be cancelled.

Refund timelines

  • Online payments are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or UPI after we confirm your details.
