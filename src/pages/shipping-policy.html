Shipping policy

Last updated 9 October 2026 · Draft for client review

Where we deliver

We deliver across India through Shiprocket and its partner couriers. Check your PIN code on any product page before you order.

Dispatch and delivery times

  • Orders are dispatched from Delhi NCR, usually within 1 to 2 working days.
  • Delhi NCR deliveries typically arrive within 2 to 3 working days of dispatch.
  • Other cities typically take 4 to 7 working days.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders of ₹499 or more after discounts. A flat ₹49 is charged on smaller orders. The exact charge is shown in your cart before you pay.

Tracking

We email and text you a tracking link when your order ships. You can also follow it on the Track order page or in My account.

Failed delivery

If a courier cannot deliver after repeated attempts, the parcel returns to us and we will contact you to re-ship or refund.
