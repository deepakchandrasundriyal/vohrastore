Our story

Distributing purity, one tin at a time

Vohra's began in Khari Baoli, the old Delhi market where India has traded dry fruits and spices for generations. Roasted makhana is how we bring that trade home to you.

Rooted in Khari Baoli, packed with care

Vohra Trading Company works from Bombay Wali Gali and Om Market in Khari Baoli, Asia's largest wholesale spice and dry-fruit market. Years of sourcing for shops across the city taught us what good makhana looks, sounds and tastes like.

Our tins are packed by RPL Dryfruit in Ballabgarh, Faridabad, in an integrated nuts and dried-fruit processing unit. Each batch is dry-roasted in rice bran oil, seasoned, and sealed in a tin that protects the crunch on its way to you.

Both the marketer and the packer are FSSAI licensed, and every tin carries its batch number, manufacturing date and best-before date.
