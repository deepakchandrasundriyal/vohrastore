All FAQs {{icon:arrow|w-4 h-4}}
What is makhana?

Makhana, also called fox nut or phool makhana, is the popped seed of the lotus plant. Roasted, it becomes a light, crunchy snack that is naturally gluten-free.

Are your makhana fried?

No. Every flavour is dry-roasted with a little rice bran oil, never deep-fried.

Which flavour has no peanut or milk?

Pink Salt is made with just three ingredients: roasted makhana, rice bran oil and Himalayan pink salt. It is produced in a facility where tree nuts and dairy may be present.

How should I store an opened tin?

Keep the lid closed and store the tin in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat to keep the makhana crisp.
